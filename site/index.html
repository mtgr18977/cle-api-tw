About

Sources and licences

Sentence excerpts shown here are short quotations from these public specifications. The CC-News reference counts come from the CC-News archive collected with news-please (Hamborg et al. 2017, doi:10.5281/zenodo.4120316). The citation is attribution, not a licence.

Limitations

  • The reference sample is small (90k articles, not random) and pruned to counts ≥ 5.
  • POS tagging of technical prose is imperfect; two documented fixes are applied.
  • GitHub has 1,534 operationIds against 1,260 distinct method+path operations; the cause was not investigated.
  • Thresholds (t ≥ 2, LL ≥ 3.84) are the plan's defaults, not yet calibrated.
